# Selection evidence and scope

Checked 17 October 2026. Chosen family: telecom-service-operations, unused in data/rebuilds.json. Distinct from field sales, helpdesk, IT service management and tonight's survey operations build. The core work is an installed service inventory and move, add, change and disconnect review.

[Salesforce Communications Cloud pricing](https://www.salesforce.com/communications/pricing/) lists Growth at US$325 and Advanced at US$475 per user per month, billed annually. Ten Advanced seats imply US$57,000 a year. This is a list-price scenario, not a verified NZ or AU customer invoice. Public copy uses the actual seat price and its annual equivalent, not an asserted customer bill.

Scores: annual bill 4, data-shaped 4, our buyers 2, search demand 2, one-session buildable 4, import path 5. Total 21/30. Local customer and CRM snapshots did not establish a named buyer of this tool. No live customer systems were queried. Search results showed a comparison listing and vendor comparisons, but keyword volume was not measured. Buyer fit and demand remain provisional.

Other unused-family candidates considered: Resolver investigation management, 1/5/2/3/4/4 = 19/30. Its [vendor pricing](https://www.resolver.com/pricing/) requires a quote, with no verified annual bill available. Sonar broadband operations, 4/2/1/2/1/2 = 12/30: its [vendor pricing](https://sonar.software/pricing) shows a five-thousand-subscriber estimate of US$75,000 per year, but its billing, payments and network operation scope makes a one-session substitute unsuitable. Learning-management software was excluded because it overlaps Enterprise DNA's own business.

Salesforce documents the industry ritual in [Manage Customer Assets with Change Orders](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/industries-cpq-asset-management/manage-customer-assets-with-change-orders). Its [report export guide](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/projects/create-reports-and-dashboards-for-sales-and-marketing-managers/manage-reported-data) supplies a CSV path. Report fields and headings are configurable; this importer requires a reviewed mapping and does not claim a universal native asset export.

The free build is a bounded internal service desk. It does not replace the full Communications Cloud platform. There is no claim that Salesforce cannot answer the included questions through custom reporting. Network provisioning, subscriber billing, usage, payments, emergency-service obligations and portals remain outside this build.
